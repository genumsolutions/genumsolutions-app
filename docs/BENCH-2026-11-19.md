## 1 · 4WD4M — U-127, the drive path's stop behaviour

The sheet is authoritative: `Genum_4WD4M_CAR/TRACKS/DEVICE-TESTS.md` U-127-1..4.
The soft-start and the project field that were missing when this sheet was written
landed on 2026-11-23; their rows are **U-131-6** and **U-131-7**. Run those two,
and use U-127-1/U-127-2 for the stop/failsafe path.

The root cause that was open: the H-bridge took full duty the same instant the
direction pins flipped, sagging the rail below the ESP32 brownout threshold.
`drive()` now registers a target and `update()` ramps to it, so that step is gone
from the source; whether it was the whole cause is what U-131-6 is for.

- [ ] **U-127-1 · repeated cycles do not reset the car.** With a live link, run
      ≥30 forward/stop cycles and ≥10 forward↔reverse cycles at full speed.
      Record whether `boot_count` / `crash_count` move or the car freezes.
- [ ] **U-127-2 · stops stay instant.** Drive forward, then send nothing (2 s
      failsafe), then try a deliberate `S` / `SPD0` / `ESTOP`. Power must cut
      immediately, not ramp down — the soft-start must never slow a stop.
- [ ] **U-127-3 · reversal is now ramped — see U-131-6.** This row used to record
      the jolt of an unramped reversal; it is superseded. On the new build, full
      forward → full reverse must show the 60 ms pause and the 250 ms climb, not
      a rail sag.
- [ ] **U-127-4 · project identity — see U-131-7.** `FW_PROJECT` ships as
      `"4wd4mcar"`; `/status` carries `"project"` and STATE carries `;PROJ=`.

**Pass =** no reboot and no freeze across the cycle run, instant failsafe on all
three stop verbs, the ramped reversal confirmed. If `crash_count` climbs, capture
`reset_reason` + `last_crash_phase` — that is `U-96g` handing you the next culprit
by name.

## 2 · 2WD1M — U-128, remembered steering/trim, servo and router switch

The sheet is authoritative: `Genum_2WD1M_CAR_NEW/TRACKS/DEVICE-TESTS.md`
U-128-1..7, which also covers the steering servo and the signed-speed path this
page used to omit.

- [ ] **U-128-1 · steering angle + trim survive a power cycle.** Save a
      non-centre steer and a non-zero trim → power-cycle the car AND restart the
      app → reconnect. Car keeps the trim, app shows the same values, and the
      trim physically moves the wheels.
- [ ] **U-128-2 · telemetry is remembered per car (app-side only).** Drive once,
      note the Telemetry tab, disconnect. Reopen with no link: previous numbers
      are shown as not live, and they are this car's (not the other car's). The
      car itself persists no telemetry, so this row tests the app, not the
      firmware.
- [ ] **U-128-3 · a Bluetooth-provisioned car joins the selected router.**
      Wi-Fi ON + BT linked (page may be OFF) → save/pick a router. The car
      leaves its own hotspot and joins it (`;SSID=` / `joined`), not merely
      stores the name.
- [ ] **U-128-4 · two routers do not mix.** Save two routers, switch twice;
      each switch lands on the requested network.
- [ ] **U-128-5 · project identity — see U-130-9.** `FW_PROJECT` ships as
      `"2wd1mcar"`; `/status` carries `"project"` and STATE carries `;PROJ=`. The
      app must group this car under its project card rather than by name.

**Pass =** values persist across a full power cycle, trim physically works, the
BT-provisioned car actually changes network, both routers usable.
