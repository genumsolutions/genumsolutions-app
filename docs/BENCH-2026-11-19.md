## 1 · 4WD4M — U-127, the reset/freeze fix

Root cause under test: the H-bridge used to take full duty the same instant the
direction pins flipped, sagging the rail below the ESP32 brownout threshold.

- [ ] **U-127-1 · repeated cycles do not reset the car.** With a live link, run
      ≥30 forward/stop cycles and ≥10 forward↔reverse cycles at full speed.
      Motion ramps up over ~250 ms instead of snapping on; `boot_count` and
      `crash_count` do not move; no freeze.
- [ ] **U-127-2 · stops stay instant.** Drive forward, then send nothing (2 s
      failsafe), then try a deliberate `S` / `SPD0` / `ESTOP`. Power must cut
      immediately, not ramp down.
- [ ] **U-127-3 · reversal is gentle.** Full forward → full reverse: the motors
      reach zero before reversing, no jolt, no reboot; the OLED `Speed` still
      reads the commanded target immediately.
- [ ] **U-127-4 · project identity.** `/status` has `"project":"4wd4mcar"`;
      a STATE line (BT and Wi-Fi) carries `;PROJ=4wd4mcar`; after an app
      restart the car still sits under that project in the device list.

**Pass =** no reboot and no freeze across the cycle run, instant failsafe,
clean reversal, three green checks. If `crash_count` climbs, capture
`reset_reason` + `last_crash_phase` — that is `U-96g` handing you the next
culprit by name.

## 2 · 2WD1M — U-128, remembered setup + router switch

- [ ] **U-128-1 · steering angle + trim survive a power cycle.** Save a
      non-centre steer and a non-zero trim → power-cycle the car AND restart the
      app → reconnect. Car keeps the trim, app shows the same values, and the
      trim physically moves the wheels.
- [ ] **U-128-2 · telemetry is remembered per car.** Drive once, note the
      Telemetry tab, disconnect. Reopen with no link: previous numbers are shown
      as not live, and they are this car's (not the other car's).
- [ ] **U-128-3 · a Bluetooth-provisioned car joins the selected router.**
      Wi-Fi ON + BT linked (page may be OFF) → save/pick a router. The car
      leaves its own hotspot and joins it (`;SSID=` / `joined`), not merely
      stores the name.
- [ ] **U-128-4 · two routers do not mix.** Save two routers, switch twice;
      each switch lands on the requested network.
- [ ] **U-128-5 · project identity.** `/status` has `"project":"2wd1mcar"`;
      STATE carries `;PROJ=2wd1mcar` on both transports; the app groups it under
      that project after a restart.

**Pass =** values persist across a full power cycle, trim physically works, the
BT-provisioned car actually changes network, both routers usable.
